Academic Programs

The university offers undergraduate programs in Computer Science, Software Engineering, and Information Technology.

The Bachelor of Computer Science program focuses on programming, algorithms, databases, artificial intelligence, software development, and computer systems.

The Bachelor of Software Engineering program focuses on software design, software testing, requirements engineering, project management, and software development.

The Bachelor of Information Technology program focuses on information systems, networking, databases, web technologies, and IT infrastructure.

Students normally complete their selected undergraduate program according to the curriculum and academic requirements established by the university.
